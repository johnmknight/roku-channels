ParkDash

About

ParkDash is a private Roku channel that shows theme park hours, ride wait times, show times, travel times and weather on a TV, styled like a resort in-room information channel. It is a non-commercial project made for family use and distributed as a Roku beta channel to a small number of TVs. It is not listed in the Roku Channel Store and is not affiliated with or endorsed by any theme park operator.

Privacy policy

ParkDash does not collect, store, sell or share any personal information about the people who use it.

  • It has no accounts, sign-in, ads, analytics or tracking.
  • All of the information it shows comes from a server on the owner's own home network, which gathers public park and weather information. Nothing about the viewer or the TV is sent to the developer or to any third party.
  • The channel does not record what is watched or how it is used.

Roku may collect information about your device and its use under Roku's own privacy policy, which this channel does not control.

Terms of use

ParkDash is provided free of charge, as is, for personal and family use, without warranty of any kind. Park hours, wait times, schedules and weather are informational only and may be incomplete or out of date; always check official sources before making plans.
