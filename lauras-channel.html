Laura's Channel

About

Laura's Channel is a private Roku channel that shows a full-screen family photo slideshow, with the date and time on screen. It is a non-commercial project made for family use and distributed as a Roku beta channel to a small number of TVs. It is not listed in the Roku Channel Store.

Privacy policy

Laura's Channel does not collect, store, sell or share any personal information about the people who use it.

  • It has no accounts, sign-in, ads, analytics or tracking.
  • The photos it shows are loaded from a server on the owner's own home network. They are not sent to the developer or to any third party.
  • The channel does not record what is watched, how it is used, or anything about the TV it runs on.

Roku may collect information about your device and its use under Roku's own privacy policy, which this channel does not control.
