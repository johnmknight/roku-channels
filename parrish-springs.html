Parrish Springs

About

Parrish Springs is a private Roku screensaver: a slow, endless stroll past illustrated small-town and theme-park-style scenes, with a day look and a night look that follow the TV's clock. It is a non-commercial project made for family use and distributed as a Roku beta channel to a small number of TVs. It is not listed in the Roku Channel Store and is not affiliated with or endorsed by any theme park operator.

Privacy policy

Parrish Springs does not collect, store, sell or share any personal information about the people who use it.

  • It has no accounts, sign-in, ads, analytics or tracking.
  • It makes no network connections; all of its artwork is built into the screensaver.
  • It does not record when or how it is shown.

Roku may collect information about your device and its use under Roku's own privacy policy, which this screensaver does not control.
