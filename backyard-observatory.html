Backyard Observatory

About

Backyard Observatory is a private Roku channel that shows, on a TV, whether tonight is a good night for backyard astronomy: sky conditions, cloud cover, the Moon's phase and position, and the hours of darkness. It is a non-commercial project made for family use and distributed as a Roku beta channel to a small number of TVs. It is not listed in the Roku Channel Store.

Privacy policy

Backyard Observatory does not collect, store, sell or share any personal information about the people who use it.

  • It has no accounts, sign-in, ads, analytics or tracking.
  • All of the information it shows comes from a server on the owner's own home network, which gathers weather forecasts and astronomical data for one fixed location. Nothing about the viewer or the TV is sent to the developer or to any third party.
  • The channel does not record what is watched or how it is used.
  • On any other network the channel cannot reach that server and shows no data.

Roku may collect information about your device and its use under Roku's own privacy policy, which this channel does not control.
